Custom liveries: what they are and how to make them

An example of a custom R/C plane livery.

What is a livery?

Simply put, a livery is a plane's paint job. While liveries are almost always done with paint on full-scale aircraft, on an R/C aircraft, they can be done with paint, sitckers, paper, or marker.

How to make a livery

Step one: print out a sheet with what you'd like to become your livery

This could be a preexisting livery from the internet, or one of your design.

Step two: print out the livery and cut it out.

Trace the outline of the plane on the paper, and cut out around the tracing line.

Step three: tape or glue the livery on.

This step should be done carefully, as any mistake could be permanent.
