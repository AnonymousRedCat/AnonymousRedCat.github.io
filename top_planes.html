My favorite R/C planes

 

1. HAWK'S WORK 2 channel cessna 170

buy on amazon

This aircraft is my overall favorite R/C aircraft and is amazing for complete beginners and children. It is a 2-channel, differential thrust-steered aircraft that is fun to fly and extremely durable. It has a long battery life and can be extensively modified. Its largest downsides are its knack for being blown around in the wind and its use of 2-channel control, which is easier but less precise than the traditional 4-channel.

 

2. HAWK'S WORK F-22

buy on amazon

This aircraft is similar to the HAWK'S WORK 170, with 2-channel control and differential thrust. The main difference between the two is the battery life, with the F-22's battery lasting only 10 minutes compared to the 170's almost 40.

 

3. FMS 1300mm Piper PA-18

buy on fmshobby.com

This is by far the largest of the aircraft, coming in at 1300mm, or over 4 feet, with a more traditional 4-channel controller. It has many more features than the other aircraft, such as flaps and control surfaces, but is much more expensive, harder to fly, and has a shorter battery life.
